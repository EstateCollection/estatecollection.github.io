SHIPPING FEES & DISCOUNTS:

We offer an excellent discounted shipping policy for customers purchasing more than item. If all unframed items can be shipped together in one tube, pay only for first item and all other items are free (highest price prevails). Although our shipping discount does not apply to framed or stretched items, we combine items whenever possible to lower shipping costs. If applicable, please await our combined invoice prior to remitting payment.

INSURANCE AND SIGNATURE REQUIRED:

We fully insure all items and, in many cases, obtain a delivery signature when deemed necessary.

INTERNATIONAL SHIPMENTS:

We offer some of the lowest rates in the industry and ship via USPS expedited service. We also combine items for discounted shipping overseas (based on the weight and dimensions of the package). If applicable, please await a combined invoice prior to remitting payment.

SHIPPING TIME:

Shipping will take place within 3 to 5 days after receiving payment from Live auctioneer.
